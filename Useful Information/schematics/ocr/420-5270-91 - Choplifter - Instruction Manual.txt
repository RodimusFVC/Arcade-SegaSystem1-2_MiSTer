NOTICE WARNING

After installation of this kit , the 5 volt power supply

must be checked and adjusted to make certain exactly

5 volts are present at pins 1 & 3 (1 is negative and

3 is positive ) of the 44 pin edge connector. The voltage

check and adjustment must be done while the PCB is

plugged in to the 44 pin connector to supply the proper

load. This PCB will not operate properly on voltages

below 5 volts as measured at the board.
